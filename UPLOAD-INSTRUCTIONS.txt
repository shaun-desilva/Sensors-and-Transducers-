PUBLISHING THIS WEBSITE UPDATE

Extract the ZIP and upload ALL extracted files and the assets folder beside index.html.
Replace the earlier index.html, app.js and styles.css, and include circuit-style.js plus every other supplied JavaScript module.
Select GitHub Actions as the Pages source; the included workflow deploys the files and assets.
Wait for deployment to succeed, then refresh the site.

The published site contains Parts 1–5, Practical Lab and Check & Reflect. Parts 6 and 7 are removed and their scripts are not required.
Do not upload the ZIP as the site, flatten assets, or omit image files.
